Pusula Medya is a space where users can share content and message each other. By creating an account and using Pusula Medya you accept the rules below.

Prohibited content

  • Hate speech, discrimination, harassment, bullying, threats and incitement to violence.
  • Any content that sexualizes or exploits children (zero tolerance; reported to the authorities).
  • Obscene, sexually explicit or extremely violent content.
  • Impersonating others or sharing their private information without consent.
  • Fraud, misleading investment/payment promises, spam and harmful links.
  • Content that infringes copyright or others' rights; illegal activities.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for what you post and must hold the necessary rights to what you upload. You give us a limited, free licence to display your content in Pusula; this ends when you delete your content.

Reports, blocking, enforcement

  • You can report any post, story, profile or message with 🚩 and block people.
  • We review reports; we may remove content that breaks the rules, restrict or close accounts, and report serious violations to the authorities. A post reported by several different people may be hidden automatically until we review it.
  • To appeal a closure, write to [YOUR EMAIL].

Communities

Community posts are public and not encrypted. Community owners and moderators can remove posts that break these rules, remove members or ban them in their communities. You can report a community with 🚩; we may also close communities that break the rules.

Live streams

Live streams are not recorded. The streamer is responsible for everything in the stream; violence, hate, nudity and copyrighted content are prohibited. You can report an inappropriate stream from the ⋯ menu; a moderator can end it immediately.

Music and sounds

You can add ready-made tunes or uploaded sounds to posts and stories. Upload only sounds you made or have the right to use; sounds that infringe copyright or contain hate or violence are removed on report. To report a sound, report the post that uses it.

Marketplace (product and service listings)

Listings are public. Posting illegal, fake, dangerous or misleading products/services, or selling weapons, drugs, stolen goods and the like, is prohibited. Pusula does not process payments and is not a party to transactions between buyer and seller; payment and delivery are entirely your responsibility. Report suspicious listings with 🚩.

Encrypted messages

Messages are end-to-end encrypted and we cannot read them. Therefore the content of a message reaches us (as evidence) only when you report that message.

Service and liability

Pusula includes an AI assistant that can make mistakes; what it says is not investment, legal, tax or medical advice. We provide the service "as is"; to the extent permitted by law we are not liable for indirect damages from interruptions or data loss. In particular, if you lose your end-to-end encrypted message keys from your device we cannot recover them.

Changes

These rules may be updated; continuing to use the app means you accept the current rules.
